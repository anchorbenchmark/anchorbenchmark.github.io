Read the research overview +
ANCHOR is a benchmark and evaluation framework for auditing perceptual grounding in multimodal reasoning. Models produce grounded chain-of-thought explanations that explicitly reference representative frames, spatial bounding boxes, and salient audio cues. The benchmark contains 2,319 videos and 8,272 question–answer pairs, including a human-refined subset of 224 videos and 975 QA pairs. Experiments expose a faithfulness gap: models that answer correctly can still produce explanations that are weakly grounded in perceptual evidence.
Scope and limitations: the full collection includes automatically generated reasoning traces; the human-refined subset is smaller. Coverage inherits the source datasets, and evaluation relies in part on model judges. See the paper for the complete protocol and limitations.
